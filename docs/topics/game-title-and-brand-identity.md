## Scope

title:

- the gates before the project adopts or announces a title.

It does **not** adopt a title, authorize a repository/package rename, reserve a

## Product Identity The Name Must Carry

Minecraft parity is a development technique and correctness oracle, not the
public identity. The intended release is an original survival-building
sandbox with first-party terrain, creatures, structures, visual assets, and
audio.

The title should arise from the combination of qualities that makes this game
specific rather than from the generic voxel genre.

### Present product and landed differentiators

- **One shared game across ordinary and spatial displays.** Desktop, web,
  Android, Quest, and desktop OpenXR are first-class clients over shared game
  and engine ownership. The name must not make XR, mobile, or any one store
  sound like the canonical edition.
- **A living original landscape.** The Mclone Overworld already has oceans,
  coasts, lowlands, uplands, mountains, valleys, rivers, wetlands, creeks,
  climate, surfaces, and vegetation. Its accepted expansion is toward richer
  regional ecology and geology; see
  [`mclone-overworld-generation.md`](mclone-overworld-generation.md) and
  [`mclone-overworld-breadth.md`](mclone-overworld-breadth.md).
- **One world visible inside another.** The live lobby diorama renders a
  persistent destination world as shared-coordinate geometry and supports a
  warm destination handoff. This is a current product capability, not merely
  concept art; see [`embedded-worlds.md`](embedded-worlds.md).
- **Worlds can have unusual exact topology.** A periodic cylinder is
  implemented across simulation, persistence, networking, and presentation;
  see [`bounded-world-topology.md`](bounded-world-topology.md).
- **An emerging creator and creature ecosystem.** Structure Lab, Asset Lab,
  Texture Lab, world-generation review tools, and the generated animal
  catalogue create room for an atlas, workshop, bestiary, and playable build
  catalogue beneath one umbrella brand.

### Accepted or exploratory north-stars

These directions legitimately inform a durable name, but must not be presented
as current launch features:

- an active-world overview that is an orbit view on a flat screen and a
  tabletop world in XR or mixed reality
  ([`tabletop-overview-mode.md`](tabletop-overview-mode.md));
- visible slab, hinge, tube, cuboid-shell, and other faithful world embeddings
  ([`faithful-world-embeddings.md`](faithful-world-embeddings.md));
- a warm, inhabited starter farmstead of farmhouse, barn, chapel, paddocks,
  water, paths, trees, and residents
  ([`starter-farmstead-settlement.md`](starter-farmstead-settlement.md)); and
- one to four local participants, auxiliary views, and mixed XR-plus-flat
  couch play ([`local-couch-multiplayer.md`](local-couch-multiplayer.md)).

### Naming synthesis

The useful naming territory is:

> A welcoming, living wilderness whose worlds can be held, nested, crossed,
> and folded.

This joins the emotional side of the game—landscape, creatures, shelter, and
belonging—to its unusual spatial side—miniatures, topology, multiple scales,
and worlds inside worlds.

## Naming Criteria

A strong title should:

- be short, pronounceable, and visually distinctive;
- suggest a living place rather than a rendering technique;
- accommodate survival, building, creatures, multiplayer, and creator tools;
- work equally well on screen, handheld, web, and headset;
- leave room for the embedded-world and topology ideas to become signature
  mechanics;
- support related names such as an Atlas, Workshop, or Bestiary;
- be searchable without relying on a subtitle; and
- have a plausibly clear domain, storefront, common-law, and trademark path.

Avoid:

- `Mine-`, `-craft`, `Block-`, `Voxel-`, or another title that makes the game
  sound like an imitation or engine demo;
- `VR`, `XR`, `Quest`, `Web`, or another platform constraint;
- selling Rust, seed parity, Java-version parity, or engine architecture;
- a generic `World`, `Realm`, or `Wilds` construction with little
  protectability; and
- a name so cozy that it excludes danger and adventure, or so hostile that it
  loses the farmstead, building, and belonging side.

## Recommendation: Wilderfold

`Wilderfold` is the strongest creative recommendation.

### Semantic fit

- **Wilder** suggests a place becoming more alive, untamed, ecological, and
  adventurous. It comfortably contains terrain generation, animals, survival,
  and exploration.
- **Fold** suggests nested or folded worlds, geological terrain folds,
  periodic geometry, and a world held at another scale.
- A **fold** is also a pastoral enclosure, most familiarly a sheepfold. That
  gives the word a quieter association with shelter, settlement, animals, and
  bringing people "into the fold."

The word therefore connects the project's two most distinctive poles:
welcoming inhabited wilderness and geometrically unusual worlds.

### Brand and language tests

The title reads naturally in product language:

- **Wilderfold**
- **Play Wilderfold**
- **Meet me in Wilderfold**
- **Wilderfold Atlas**
- **Wilderfold Workshop**
- **Wilderfold Bestiary**

Promising message tests include:

- **A living world at every scale.**
- **Build a world. Step inside.**
- **Make a home in worlds that fold.**
- **One world, every way to play.**

A current descriptive pitch is:

> **Wilderfold is a living survival-building sandbox where worlds can be
> explored, held in miniature, and stepped inside—on screen or in spatial
> play.**

This is positioning language, not a locked tagline. In particular, public copy
must distinguish present live-diorama traversal from the later tabletop and
faithful-embedding north-stars.

### Creative concerns

- The first two syllables and indie-fantasy cadence faintly recall
  [Wildermyth](https://store.steampowered.com/app/763890/Wildermyth/), an
  established game, even though the complete words, meanings, mechanics, and
  genres differ.
- `fold` can also mean closing or surrendering. The landscape, world, and
  shelter readings are stronger in the proposed visual and verbal context,
  but player testing should verify that.
- The pronunciation and associations have not been screened across target
  languages.

Creatively, the recommendation is green. Legally and operationally, it remains
amber until the clearance gates are complete.

## Preliminary Availability Research

Research was performed on **2026-07-23**. It is a point-in-time screen for
obvious problems, not a legal opinion or a promise that a registrar will still
offer a name.

### Exact-name and domain screen

For `Wilderfold`, exact quoted web searches and exact-title searches across
Steam, itch.io, game/studio/software terms, and public trademark-index results
did not surface an exact game, studio, software product, or obvious exact U.S.
word mark.

The following registry-data lookups returned HTTP `404` / not found:

- `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/WILDERFOLD.COM`
- `https://rdap.org/domain/wilderfold.game`

The same `.com` and `.game` checks returned not found for `Wilderspan`,
`Loamward`, `Ravelwild`, and `WorldsAfield`.

RDAP is the authoritative registration-data protocol for generic top-level
domains, but a not-found response is only evidence that the registry did not
return a current registration at the time of the query. ICANN's
[RDAP transition notice](https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-update-launching-rdap-sunsetting-whois-27-01-2025-en)
describes RDAP as the definitive gTLD registration-data source from January
2025.

### Similar-name concerns

Exact availability is not sufficient:

- **WILDFOLD** is a live U.S. registration associated with portable camping
  and appliance goods. The currently indexed record is serial
  `99378822` in international class 11; see the
  [official USPTO TSDR
  record](https://tsdr.uspto.gov/#caseNumber=99378822&caseSearchType=US_APPLICATION&caseType=DEFAULT&searchType=statusSearch)
  and
  [Justia record](https://trademarks.justia.com/993/78/wildfold-99378822.html).
  Its goods appear unrelated to games, but the word differs from `Wilderfold`
  by only two letters.
- **WonderFold** is an established stroller-wagon brand; see
  [WonderFold's company page](https://wonderfold.com/pages/about-1).
- **Wildermyth** is an established video game in the same broad entertainment
  market; see [its Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/763890/Wildermyth/).
  The shared `Wilder-` opening creates a brand-positioning concern even if it
  is not ultimately a legal conflict.
- **Ravelwild** appears as an unrelated wallpaper colorway in
  [Hirshfield's coverage of the Partridge & Willow
  collection](https://www.hirshfields.com/wallpaper-wednesday-partridge-and-willow/).

The USPTO warns that marks need not be identical: similarity in sound,
appearance, meaning, or commercial impression can matter when the goods or
services are related. Its
[likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)
and
[comprehensive-clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
are the standard for the next U.S. step.

### Names rejected because of obvious occupancy

- **Hearthfold** — existing
  [Hearthfold Games](https://www.hearthfoldgames.com/) and other current
  creative uses.
- **Worldfold** — existing
  [Worldfold game on itch.io](https://araarathain.itch.io/worldfold).
- **Wildfold** — the live U.S. registration noted above.
- **Wilderstead** — active homesteading identity at
  [Wilderstead](https://www.wilderstead.com/).
- **Horizonfold** — active company at
  [HorizonFold](https://horizonfold.com/).
- **Landloom** — already used by a real-estate analytics product when
  researched.
- **Elsefield** — active commercial identity and registered `.com`.
- **Worldstead**, **Patchwild**, and **Wildseam** — `.com` registrations
  already existed at the time of checking.
- **Fablefold**, **Hinterfold**, **Foldhaven**, **Mossmantle**, and
  **Seamlands** — existing creative, commercial, fictional-place, or community
  uses made them weaker than the retained shortlist.

### 2026-07-24 fresh-candidate round

A second generation-and-screen round was run on **2026-07-24** after the
`-fold` shortlist failed to resonate. Same method as above: authoritative RDAP
`.com` lookups (a `404` means no current registration), followed by exact
quoted web/Steam/itch.io searches for game, studio, and brand conflicts. Same
caveats apply — this is a point-in-time screen, not legal clearance.

`Woldenmere` emerged as the maintainer's preferred contender from this round:

- **Semantic fit.** It parses as *wold* (Old English *weald/wald* "forest,"
  later "high open upland"; cognate German *Wald*, Dutch *woud*) plus *-mere*
  (Old English *mere* "lake"; cognate Latin *mare*, German *Meer*, Dutch
  *meer*). It reads as a real North-Sea place-name — "a lake by the wooded
  upland" — rather than invented fantasy, which suits the living-landscape
  identity. The `-mere` lake element is familiar from Windermere, Grasmere,
  and Buttermere.
- **Cross-language legibility.** The name is transparent and benign to a
  German/Swiss-German/Dutch ear (decodes as *Wäld(li)* + *Meer/meer*), which
  partially discharges the target-language screening gate on the Germanic side.
  Note the shared root split in meaning: English/Dutch *mere/meer* settled on
  "lake" while German *Meer* settled on "sea," so a German reading lands on
  "forest-sea" and an English reading on "forest-lake"; both resolve to wooded
  water and neither is an unfortunate homophone.
- **Screen result.** `WOLDENMERE.COM` returned RDAP not-found, and exact
  searches surfaced no existing video game, studio, or obvious brand — only
  unrelated geographic adjacents (Windermere, De Wolden). It carries no
  `Wilder-`/`-fold` cadence, so it reads as a clean reset rather than a
  Wilderfold variation.

Other `.com`-clear, conflict-screened candidates from the same round, retained
as secondary options:

- `Loamere`, `Woldenholt` — same earthy real-place register as `Woldenmere`.
- `Nestholt` — the only candidate that encodes the nested-worlds hook (*nest*)
  and the wilderness (*holt*, a copse) together.
- `Kithvale`, `Roostvale` — the warm/belonging pole (*kith* = one's land and
  people; *roost* = a home returned to).

Round rejects worth recording so they are not re-proposed: `Bramblen` (a
homophone of the Pokémon *Bramblin*, which buries it in search); `Cradlelands`
(too close to the survival-crafting game *Crashlands*); `Brackenmere` (an
existing fantasy-fiction setting); `Verdanni` (near *Verdandi* and an ESG
company); `Hearthmoss` (crowded `Hearth-` space plus a live streamer handle);
`Holtaria` (near-identical to the live corporate brand *Holtara*). Every rare
single real word tested (`bield`, `thwaite`, `garth`, `weald`, `combe`, and
similar) had its `.com` already registered, confirming that this constraint
pushes strongly toward coined words and non-`-fold` compounds.

## Clearance And Adoption Gates

Before adopting or announcing `Wilderfold`:

1. Confirm that the creative direction is accepted after saying, spelling, and
   showing the name to representative players.
2. Commission a comprehensive similarity and common-law search for the
   relevant software, downloadable-game, entertainment, online-service, and
   creator-tool goods and services. At minimum, cover the United States,
   European Union, United Kingdom, and intended early sales territories.
3. Have counsel assess **WILDFOLD**, **Wildermyth**, **WonderFold**, and any
   additional phonetic, visual, or semantic results. Search classes and related
   goods rather than assuming a different class is automatically safe.
4. Recheck and reserve the chosen domains and priority social/store handles
   before public disclosure. Domain availability alone must not decide the
   title.
5. Screen pronunciation, meaning, and undesirable associations in target
   languages.
6. Decide the mark owner and filing strategy before commissioning expensive
   public identity work.
7. Only after adoption, plan the separate user-facing copy, package, app ID,
   website, store, repository, and crate/package rename. Internal historical
   terms and Minecraft reference paths should not be mechanically rewritten.

If `Wilderfold` fails clearance, return first to **Wilderspan**, then
**Loamward**, while preserving the same product-identity brief. A fresh
candidate round should explore the same living-landscape plus cross-scale
world territory rather than falling back to `Mine`, `Craft`, `Block`, or
`Voxel`.
