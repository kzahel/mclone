# Tactical 334: Cross-Profile Wildlife Habitat Routing

Status: **proposed 2026-08-29; selected as the ecology-routing prerequisite
for Tactical 332. The profile-neutral policy and planner extraction may begin
before Mclone Overworld V3 is complete, but V3 ecology enablement or terrain
changes remain gated on Human Review V3-A.**

## Instruction Synthesis

Make every promoted creature usable in worlds made by any supported terrain
profile. Retire the accidental rule that Mclone's habitat-driven population
exists only in `mclone-overworld-v1`, without replacing it with species-blind
random spawning. V1 is an internal experiment and must not remain the hidden
owner of reusable creature behavior, population identity, or habitat policy.

Separate terrain generation from wildlife policy. Each profile may publish
different semantic evidence, and some worlds may genuinely lack habitat for a
specialist, but no creature kind is rejected merely because the world was
generated by V2, V3, Flat Grass, Small Island, a historical profile, or the
legacy Java-shaped Overworld. Use deterministic randomness to choose among
viable sites and species; never use randomness to overrule water, support,
food, cover, collision, or other hard habitat facts.

Do not wait for a feature-complete V3. Extract the reusable ecology boundary
now, adapt the habitat facts V2 and V3 already provide, and let subsequent
creature chapters expose concrete missing terrain capabilities. Frogs should
be the first such V3 chapter after its terrain review: basin lakes should gain
reusable shallow living margins, bank cover, and aquatic opportunity useful to
mallards and future water life as well as frogs.

## Product Question

Can the same authoritative wildlife population and durable creature lifecycle
operate across every ordinary world-generation profile, selecting animals from
truthful profile-neutral habitat evidence and final generated blocks, while
remaining deterministic, bounded, persistent, and honestly empty where the
terrain supplies no suitable habitat?

## Decision

Adopt one profile-neutral habitat-driven wildlife policy for ordinary worlds.
World generation supplies terrain; a separate persisted wildlife policy says
whether natural founding is allowed; shared species rules score habitat; and
the server validates final blocks before materializing durable creatures.

"Available in any world" has three precise meanings:

1. **Material availability:** a promoted creature kind may be authored,
   summoned, transported, saved, loaded, ticked, replicated, and rendered in
   any dimension or generation profile. No behavior or persistence code may
   reject it by terrain-profile identity.
2. **Population eligibility:** a dimension whose stored wildlife policy opts
   into habitat-driven founding considers the complete promoted natural
   roster. The generator profile does not silently substitute another roster.
3. **Site viability:** a particular species appears naturally only where
   shared habitat rules and final live blocks support it. An unsuitable cell
   remains empty; there is no guarantee that every species appears in every
   seed, region, or untouched terrain recipe.

This tactical changes founding and routing, not the established active ecology
model. Materialized animals remain durable individuals. Empty saved entity
records remain authoritative. There is no periodic refill, desired population
floor, hidden immigration, or unloaded catch-up.

## Current Failure

The implementation currently has two complementary profile checks rather than
one explicit policy:

- `initial_wildlife_population_enabled` admits the deterministic Mclone
  population only when the active profile is exactly
  `McloneOverworldV1`;
- `McloneOverworldWildlifePlanner` directly owns the V1 terrain sampler and V1
  vegetation planner;
- the existing population mix is rabbit, deer, mallard, bee, and squirrel;
- the older cadence-driven live passive path is enabled for an unbounded
  profile whenever that profile is *not* V1, so ordinary V2 and V3 worlds can
  fall into the Java-shaped cow/chicken route despite being original Mclone
  profiles; and
- Terrain Lab's exact production wildlife pane is V1-only.

This is a routing accident, not a defensible ecology boundary. V2 already
publishes rich forest, clearing, wetland, climate, drainage, water, and refuge
facts. V3 already publishes landform, water kind and level, substrate,
moisture, openness, forest opportunity, and stable tree records. They are
behind in integration, not devoid of habitat. V3 is also intentionally behind
in flower and wetland-margin vocabulary, which should remain visible as an
honest content gap rather than be masked by random placement.

## Binding Contracts

### Wildlife policy is not a generator profile

Add a versioned, persisted `WildlifePopulationPolicy` (final code name may
vary) to `DimensionDefinition` and its durable `DimensionRecord`, beside but
not inside `WorldGenerationProfile`. Carry it through any current top-level
world-metadata compatibility mirror and through integrated, dedicated,
memory/null, SQLite, and IndexedDB startup paths. It must be resolved before
entity-chunk realization.

The first policy set is:

- `Disabled`: no automatic initial wildlife or live passive producer;
- `HabitatDrivenV1`: the complete promoted natural roster uses the shared
  deterministic founder planner and habitat adapters; and
- `ReferencePassiveV1`: an explicit internal/reference lane for the existing
  cadence-driven cow/chicken planner when that behavior is still useful for a
  bounded comparison.

`ReferencePassiveV1` is not inferred from `WorldGenerationProfile::Overworld`
and is not the default for ordinary world creation. A single dimension may
have at most one automatic population producer. The runtime natural-spawning
master switch may pause the selected producer, but it must not redefine which
producer the saved world owns.

New user-selectable procedural worlds default to `HabitatDrivenV1`, including
Mclone V1/V2/V3, legacy Overworld, Flat Grass, Small Island, Alpha temperate,
Alpha winter, and Beta. `AuthoredOnly` and the hidden `TopologyProbeV1` default
to `Disabled` so exact fixtures do not acquire unsolicited animals; they may
opt in explicitly when a test, tool, or authored world wants ordinary natural
founding.

The initial automatic scope remains the Overworld dimension. Other dimensions
may contain, transport, persist, and simulate every creature, but enabling
natural founding there requires an explicit dimension policy and habitat
acceptance rather than inheriting the realm's surface-world choice.

### One promoted natural roster

`HabitatDrivenV1` considers every creature already promoted through a real
natural producer:

- cow;
- chicken;
- rabbit;
- deer;
- mallard;
- bee; and
- squirrel.

Tactical 332 adds frog to this same roster. Future creature chapters must add
one shared species rule and exact-placement rule rather than a profile gate or
another periodic spawn loop. Asset Lab figures that have not been promoted to
ordinary gameplay do not enter the roster merely because art exists.

Cow and chicken move into deterministic habitat-driven founding during this
tactical instead of remaining a second generic live population. Cow favors
productive supported open ground; chicken favors supported dry ground with
seed/low-cover opportunity. A later domestication decision may change whether
either belongs in wild founding, but the current behavior remains available
without owning a separate scheduler.

### Candidate geography is shared; evidence is adapted

Extract a profile-neutral `WildlifePopulationPlanner` from the V1-owned
planner. It retains the useful current shape:

```text
seed + topology + policy revision + 64x64 population cell
  -> fixed coordinate-pure candidate sites
  -> profile adapter supplies broad habitat evidence
  -> shared species rules produce viability and weights
  -> deterministic occupancy and weighted-species rolls
  -> one owner chunk and bounded group intent, or an empty cell
  -> final live-block placement validation
  -> ordinary durable entities and an authoritative entity record
```

Candidate positions, random lanes, occupancy, species selection, group size,
and planned identity inputs are shared. A profile adapter may not choose a
species or add its own random stream. Species rules may not query a concrete
profile enum.

The current V1 result should remain an exact extraction canary before the
roster/revision change. Adding cow and chicken or deliberately changing
weights requires a population revision bump and updated distribution receipts;
it must not be hidden inside the mechanical refactor.

### Habitat evidence is profile-neutral and truth-bearing

Define one bounded internal `WildlifeHabitatEvidence` value sufficient for
population scoring. It is not a public mod API and does not promise that every
generator computes every fact natively. The first vocabulary should include:

- surface elevation, land/water identity, support, local slope, and exposure;
- temperature, moisture, productivity, and seasonal-policy availability;
- openness, low cover, forest cover, forest edge, and mature-tree evidence;
- flowering opportunity and seed or soft-mast opportunity;
- inland water, water depth class, shore adjacency, wetland strength, and bank
  accessibility;
- aquatic-resource opportunity and shallow refuge/landing opportunity;
- evidence provenance, completeness, and current availability; and
- topology/bounds facts needed to interpret an edge or canonical lift.

Do not turn missing evidence into zero. An adapter returns available evidence,
currently unavailable evidence, or an explicit unsupported-policy error. A
planner may defer on unavailable inputs; it may reject on observed unsuitable
facts; it may not consult V1 as a fallback for another profile.

Broad evidence proposes a founder group. Final generated blocks remain the
authority for collision, support, water depth, reachable landing space,
flowers, trees, cover, and other material requirements. A semantic wetland
fact cannot place a frog in deep water; a biome label cannot place a bee where
no flowering resource can be reached.

### Each profile supplies facts through a narrow adapter

The first adapter matrix is:

| profile family | broad evidence source | expected initial result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mclone V1 | current terrain and vegetation samples | exact extraction baseline, then shared roster |
| Mclone V2 | `ContinentalSurfaceSample` and stable vegetation/water facts | rich direct ecology with live-block checks |
| Mclone V3 | accepted `V3TerrainSample`, water, moisture, openness, forest opportunity, and stable trees | general land/woodland first; specialists stay conservative until terrain supports them |
| legacy Overworld, Alpha, Beta, Small Island | canonical generator/biome summaries plus bounded generated-block evidence | shared roster scored from actual terrain, not V1 semantics |
| Flat Grass | simple generator facts plus bounded generated-block evidence | honest open-ground opportunity; specialists may remain absent |
| Authored Only | bounded active block evidence when explicitly opted in | authored habitat can qualify without a procedural sampler |
| Topology Probe | probe facts only when explicitly opted in | conformance evidence, never a product population promise |

Prefer direct canonical generator queries when a profile already owns them.
For simple, historical, or authored profiles, add one bounded block-derived
adapter over immutable published chunk snapshots. It may inspect only a fixed
candidate neighborhood and must declare which snapshots are required. It may
not force-load arbitrary chunks, expand its search after failure, depend on
player traversal order, or replace unavailable chunks with air.

The block-derived path is an evidence fallback, not a habitat fallback. It lets
an oak, flower patch, pond, or constructed shore count because it exists; it
does not manufacture a pond, flower, tree, or animal when none exists.

### Randomness creates variation inside ecology

Retain deterministic randomness for:

- candidate positions within a population cell;
- whether a sufficiently productive cell receives an encounter;
- weighted selection among species whose hard requirements pass;
- bounded group size, orientation, and planned identity inputs; and
- choosing among several equally valid exact placements.

Do not add:

- `if no species qualifies, pick any creature`;
- a minimum encounter count per profile or biome;
- retries in adjacent cells until something spawns;
- profile-wide guaranteed representation for specialists;
- a default grass-only placement for water, canopy, flower, or bank species;
  or
- a generic periodic refill after initial realization.

Generalists may have deliberately broad habitat weights. That is a species
rule, not an escape hatch. Specialists keep hard requirements. Empty cells and
locally absent species are legitimate ecological content and diagnostic
evidence.

### Persistence remains the final authority

First entity-chunk realization keeps the Tactical 298 contract:

- a missing entity record may run the selected deterministic founding plan;
- a present record, including an empty one, suppresses all seed re-planning;
- exact-placement failure completes the bounded attempt and persists the empty
  result rather than searching forever;
- existing animals survive policy migration with the same persistent IDs; and
- changing a policy or planner revision never resurrects, replaces, or
  duplicates animals in already realized entity chunks.

For old internal worlds without the new metadata field, decode a documented
one-time default and persist it on the next metadata write. The compatibility
ledger permits intentional changes, but migration must still be deterministic
and tested. Existing V2/V3 cows or chickens created by the accidental live
route remain ordinary durable entities; removing that producer does not remove
them.

### Terrain improvements are creature-driven and reusable

Do not delay cross-profile routing until V3 has every desired biome or habitat.
Enable the adapter against accepted facts and let unsupported species weights
remain low or zero. A creature chapter may then add the smallest missing
terrain capability that makes its gameplay legible.

The first V3 ecology improvement belongs to Tactical 332 after Human Review
V3-A. It should extend basin lakes with a broadly reusable shallow living
margin: variable shallows, low reachable banks, wet cover, and truthful
aquatic-resource opportunity. The facts must benefit mallards, frogs, insects,
shore plants, and later fish or beaver work. They must follow V3's accepted
landform and water structure rather than reshape a mountain, fill a valley, or
read a frog-only noise field.

V2 initially receives an adapter, not automatic terrain surgery. If profile
diagnostics show that its rich semantic wetlands or forests are not realized
as usable blocks, repair that semantic-to-block mismatch in a focused shared
terrain slice justified by all affected species.

## Ownership

- `mclone-server` owns the persisted wildlife policy, single-producer routing,
  entity-record admission, live-block evidence, exact placement, durable
  materialization, and diagnostics.
- `mclone-worldgen` owns coordinate-pure candidate geography, profile-neutral
  habitat evidence types, shared species weights, direct profile adapters, and
  deterministic distribution receipts.
- each generator source owns only its truthful terrain-to-evidence mapping; it
  does not own species selection or server persistence.
- `DimensionDefinition` and the server persistence codecs carry the neutral
  persisted descriptor. A temporary top-level `WorldMetadata` mirror may
  exist only where the current legacy-overworld migration requires it.
- Terrain Lab presents the exact production plan and adapter evidence through
  the existing Rust/Wasm Worker path. TypeScript remains presentation only.
- protocol, clients, renderers, audio, and platform apps receive ordinary
  entity state. They do not select a wildlife policy or reinterpret habitat.

## Phases And Commit Gates

### Phase 0: Baseline, review boundary, and contracts

1. Land this tactical and update the living ecology, profile, and worldgen
   direction.
2. Record exact current V1 population receipts and the V2/V3 accidental
   cow/chicken live-routing behavior as regression evidence.
3. Record the V3-A human decision when available. Shared extraction may
   proceed while it is pending; V3 ecology enablement and terrain edits may
   not.
4. Define the policy, evidence, adapter, roster, availability, and revision
   types without changing runtime behavior.

**Gate A:** tests lock the existing two routes and V1 plan, and review accepts
the distinction between material availability, population eligibility, and
site viability.

### Phase 1: Persisted policy and single-producer routing

1. Add the versioned wildlife policy to `DimensionDefinition`, its durable
   record, any required compatibility metadata, catalog descriptors, startup
   codecs, persistence, and diagnostics.
2. Apply the documented defaults to new procedural, authored, and probe
   worlds; add deterministic missing-field migration for internal saves.
3. Replace complementary `== V1` / `!= V1` checks with one exhaustive policy
   dispatch.
4. Keep `ReferencePassiveV1` callable only through its explicit policy and
   prove that no dimension can run both producers.

**Gate B:** memory/null, SQLite, IndexedDB, integrated, dedicated, and catalog
round trips agree on profile plus wildlife policy. V2 and V3 no longer enter a
Java-shaped live route merely because they are not V1.

### Phase 2: Shared planner and habitat evidence

1. Extract candidate geography, rolls, encounter ownership, and shared species
   scoring from `McloneOverworldWildlifePlanner`.
2. Preserve exact V1 results through the mechanical extraction.
3. Define direct and block-derived adapter contracts with explicit
   available/unavailable/unsupported outcomes and bounded dependencies.
4. Move cow and chicken into the promoted natural roster, bump the population
   revision, and recalibrate deterministic broad surveys without quotas.

**Gate C:** order, partition, negative-coordinate, cache-reset, topology, and
V1 extraction tests pass. The new roster produces bounded multi-seed receipts;
dry, ocean, alpine, or otherwise unsupported cells may remain empty.

### Phase 3: Profile adapters and exact placement

1. Implement V1, V2, and accepted-fact V3 adapters without cross-calling
   another profile's source.
2. Implement the bounded generator/biome/block adapter for legacy Overworld,
   Alpha, Beta, Small Island, Flat Grass, and opted-in Authored Only.
3. Route every species through shared final block validation and fixed
   placement-candidate limits.
4. Persist an empty result after an observed unsuitable or exhausted exact
   attempt; defer, without concluding, while required evidence is unavailable.

**Gate D:** every ordinary procedural profile runs `HabitatDrivenV1` through
one producer. Positive witnesses show the habitats each profile actually has;
negative witnesses prove no specialist fallback and no unavailable-as-empty
load race.

### Phase 4: Terrain Lab and distribution review

1. Generalize Terrain Lab's Wildlife pane to the global profile selector.
2. Show wildlife policy, adapter/provenance, evidence completeness, hard
   viability failures, weights, rolls, selected encounter, and final-placement
   status where live evidence exists.
3. Add a reproducible cross-profile atlas for matched seeds and comparable
   footprints, plus profile-specific characteristic sites where appropriate.
4. Inspect desktop and phone maps and keep Worker cell counts, stale epochs,
   and source ownership bounded.

**Gate E:** a reviewer can distinguish an empty occupancy roll, unsuitable
habitat, unavailable evidence, a disabled policy, and an unsupported adapter.
V1, V2, and V3 maps are visibly derived from their own facts.

### Phase 5: Runtime, persistence, and platform closeout

1. Visit, unload, reload, close, and reopen founder chunks across representative
   V1, V2, V3, legacy, simple, and authored/block-derived profiles.
2. Prove stable IDs, empty-record authority, policy migration, no dual
   producer, no seed resurrection, and null-store once-per-session behavior.
3. Run existing ecology campaigns against unchanged V1 controls, then add
   cross-profile short runs as calibration evidence rather than population
   targets.
4. Build and exercise native, Web/Wasm, flat Android, XR, and dedicated-server
   boundaries affected by metadata or roster changes.
5. Update this execution record and living docs before Tactical 332 begins.

**Gate F:** all supported clients consume the same authoritative population;
profile selection changes habitat evidence only, never client behavior or
creature availability.

### Phase 6: Frog-driven V3 wetland handoff

This is a handoff gate, not frog implementation inside this tactical.

1. Produce a V1/V2/V3 frog-readiness report from the shared diagnostics.
2. Identify the smallest accepted V3 basin-lake margin facts and blocks needed
   by frogs and already-useful mallard/aquatic-resource behavior.
3. Amend Tactical 332's starting baselines with those profile witnesses.
4. Leave chorus, brood, frog lifecycle, assets, and terrain realization to
   Tactical 332.

**Gate G:** Tactical 332 begins from profile-neutral population routing and a
reviewed multi-species V3 habitat brief, not from a V1 planner fork or random
fallback.

## Validation Matrix

At minimum, cover:

- metadata defaults, malformed policy rejection, old-field migration, binary
  and JSON codec round trips, and profile/policy independence;
- exhaustive single-producer dispatch, runtime master-disable behavior, and
  explicit reference-lane selection;
- exact V1 extraction parity before the deliberate population revision;
- deterministic candidate sites, rolls, ownership, group size, identity
  inputs, negative coordinates, order, partition, and cache independence;
- each adapter's mapping, provenance, completeness, unavailable outcome,
  topology/bounds handling, and prohibition on V1 cross-sampling;
- cow, chicken, rabbit, deer, mallard, bee, and squirrel positive and hard-
  negative species rules;
- exact support, collision, water-depth, shore, flower, tree, food, and cover
  validation with a fixed retry bound;
- no encounter on all-zero suitability, no minimum profile count, and no
  species-blind fallback under multi-seed surveys;
- missing versus empty entity records, failed-placement empty persistence,
  stable reopen IDs, old V2/V3 animal preservation, and no resurrection;
- transient/null once-per-session behavior and unavailable snapshot deferral;
- Terrain Lab production-source ownership, bounded Worker work, reproducible
  URLs, profile switching, desktop/phone interaction, and inspected pixels;
- native/Wasm equivalence, SQLite/IndexedDB reopen, dedicated-server startup,
  flat Android and Android XR builds, and relevant mono/stereo/multiview
  presentation canaries; and
- unchanged active-tick, seasonal-resource, population-conservation, and
  graceful-overload invariants after founding.

Rendered Terrain Lab and in-world milestones require captures under `/tmp` and
visual inspection. A map proves population decisions; a live persistence gate
proves material creatures. Neither substitutes for the other.

## Explicit Deferrals

- periodic population refill, immigration, recolonization, desired counts,
  population floors, or unloaded ecology simulation;
- guaranteeing every species in every seed, region, biome, profile, or
  untouched world;
- changing an unsuitable cell into habitat during population planning;
- a public plugin/mod habitat API or dynamic species registry;
- migration routes, predator-prey population summaries, or remote habitat
  indexing;
- natural founding in Nether-like or other non-Overworld dimensions without an
  explicit later policy;
- exposing the reference passive policy as a normal product-facing world
  option;
- frog assets, chorus, brood, lifecycle, or V3 wetland realization, which
  remain in Tactical 332; and
- making V3 the default, retiring V1/V2, or resolving V3-A by documentation.
